How to play

  • 1Look at the target swatch. A random color appears at the top.
  • 2Drag the R, G, B sliders to mix your own version on the right. Your mix updates live.
  • 3Stuck? Tap a __ pair on the target hex to use your hint and reveal that channel.
  • 4Out of hints? Tap "+ Earn" for an Odd-One-Out challenge. Spot the different square in 5 seconds to earn a hint.
  • 5Hit Submit Mix. Your accuracy becomes coins to spend in the Wardrobe!
🌈 What's RGB? (new to color mixing?)

Screens make every color by mixing three lights: Red, Green, and Blue. Each slider controls how bright that light is, from 0 (off) to 255 (full).

R: 255
R+G
All on
All off

Unlike paint (where mixing makes things darker), light mixing makes things brighter. R + G + B at full = white.
